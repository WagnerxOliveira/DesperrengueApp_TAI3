## [v2.12.0+202611141] — 2026-11-14 — Founder Sovereign Cockpit & Dual-APK Zero-Trust Architecture ([`ADR-0023`](docs/adr/0023-founder-sovereign-cockpit-dual-apk.md))

### Adicionado
- **Cofre Soberano Zero-Trust no PostgreSQL 17 ([`20261114100000_founder_sovereign_cockpit_zero_trust.sql`](supabase/migrations/20261114100000_founder_sovereign_cockpit_zero_trust.sql) & [`founder_zero_trust_cockpit_smoke.sql`](supabase/tests/founder_zero_trust_cockpit_smoke.sql), SHA `2e91794`):**
  - Tabelas blindadas `private.founder_sovereign_acl`, `private.founder_stepup_sessions` e `private.founder_audit_log` (`RLS` + `FORCE RLS` + `REVOKE ALL FROM PUBLIC, anon, authenticated`).
  - Unicidade vitalícia `SOVEREIGN_CEO` (`uq_single_sovereign_ceo`), trava física via trigger exclusiva limitando convidados a **no máximo 3 delegados ativos** (`ERR_MAX_3_AUTHORIZED_DELEGATES_EXCEEDED`), **6 RPCs `SECURITY DEFINER`** com Step-Up PIN (`bcrypt` + token efêmero `SHA-256`), *Kill-Switch* imediato de sessão, cadeia imutável WORM `SHA-256` e snapshot analítico somente-leitura (`SET LOCAL transaction_read_only = on`) com índices *covering* `INCLUDE`.
- **Contabilidade CPC 47 / IFRS 15 em Centavos Inteiros (`BIGINT`), 5 Motores de Programação Dinâmica, Monte Carlo Off-Thread e Exportador `%PDF-1.4` ([`founder_dashboard_models.dart`](app/lib/core/analytics/founder_dashboard_models.dart), [`founder_dp_optimization_engine.dart`](app/lib/core/analytics/founder_dp_optimization_engine.dart) & [`founder_accountant_pack_service.dart`](app/lib/core/analytics/founder_accountant_pack_service.dart), SHA `f48fd04`):**
  - DRE CPC 47 Principal vs. Agente em centavos inteiros (`0` deriva de ponto flutuante, Simples Nacional Anexo III $6\%$ apenas sobre a receita própria de intermediação) e Reconciliação Tripla (`pix_topups` vs. `ledger_lines` vs. `wallets + escrow`) sensível a `1 centavo`.
  - 5 motores determinísticos de Programação Dinâmica (`PrefixSumFinancialTimeline` $O(1)$, `KnapsackGrowthAndBoosterOptimizer` Mochila 0/1, `MarkovBellmanCohortLtvEngine` Bellman LTV/CAC por coorte, `KadaneAndCusumAnomalyDetector` Kadane $O(T)$ + Page CUSUM e `AntiBypassAndLeakageDetector` Wagner-Fischer anti-evasão).
  - Simulação de Monte Carlo (`1.000` trajetórias $\times$ 12 meses via `runOffMainThread` com `Mulberry32` + Box-Muller) e exportação vetorial `%PDF-1.4` + CSV + `SHA-256` para o Pacote Contábil Mensal CPC 47 e o Dossiê Policial Anti-Coação (`FROZEN_UNDER_DURESS`).
- **Cockpit Executivo *Obsidian Sovereign Luxury* a 120 FPS ([`main_founder.dart`](app/lib/main_founder.dart), [`founder_cockpit_screen.dart`](app/lib/features/founder/presentation/founder_cockpit_screen.dart), [`founder_charts_painters.dart`](app/lib/features/founder/presentation/founder_charts_painters.dart), [`founder_innovations_widgets.dart`](app/lib/features/founder/presentation/founder_innovations_widgets.dart), [`founder_sovereign_acl_panel.dart`](app/lib/features/founder/presentation/founder_sovereign_acl_panel.dart), [`founder_animations_and_shaders.dart`](app/lib/features/founder/presentation/founder_animations_and_shaders.dart) & [`founder_cockpit_repository.dart`](app/lib/features/founder/data/founder_cockpit_repository.dart), SHAs `9af18ca`, `c212935` e `6aec511`):**
